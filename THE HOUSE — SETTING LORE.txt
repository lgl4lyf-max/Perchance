This information defines the physical structure, layout, property, furnishings, landscaping, lighting, and established features of the House. Treat it as authoritative.

Do not alter, relocate, remove, or invent rooms, structures, furnishings, doors, windows, landscaping, lighting, or other physical features.

HOUSE OVERVIEW

The House is a large, secluded, single-story ranch home styled like a rustic Aspen ski resort lodge. It is nestled deep within a thick hardwood forest.

A long gravel driveway winds approximately 150 feet from the main road to a spacious circular driveway.

At the center of the circular driveway is a large rock and flower garden. A large mature oak tree grows around a granite boulder in the center of this garden.

A slate sidewalk leads from the circular driveway to a wide front porch.

A second, separate rock and flower garden surrounds the front of the house, extending along the front facade and occupying the landscaped areas between the house and the circular driveway. The garden does not occupy the areas directly in front of the garage doors, which require unobstructed access.

There is no grass in the front yard. Front landscaping consists of the gravel driveway, rock and flower gardens, mature trees, and other established landscaping described here.

The property contains a 3-car attached garage and a 4-car detached garage used as a workshop.

Dense hedgerows surround the backyard and block access from the surrounding forest. The backyard can be entered from the house or through a locked wooden gate.

EXTERIOR / NIGHTTIME LIGHTING

The House and property have a carefully designed landscape-lighting system.

Hidden, upward-facing lights are positioned throughout the property and shine into the surrounding tree canopy.

At night, these concealed lights illuminate the trunks and upper branches of the surrounding hardwood trees, creating a soft, diffuse, ethereal glow throughout the front and backyard.

The lighting is intentionally subtle rather than harsh or floodlit. The goal is to illuminate the forest canopy and create an atmospheric glow while preserving the feeling of being surrounded by a dark forest.

The rock and flower gardens have their own dedicated landscape lighting.

Individual lights are positioned to highlight selected trees, rocks, flowers, landscaping features, and architectural elements.

The garden lighting is selective rather than uniformly bright. Particular features stand out against the darker surrounding landscape.

The combined canopy lighting and garden lighting give the property a distinctive appearance at night: the House and gardens are softly illuminated while the surrounding forest remains dark beyond the property.

The nighttime lighting is present throughout both the front and backyard.

BACKYARD — SIX LAYERS

The backyard is organized into six distinct layers, progressing outward from the house toward the surrounding forest.

LAYER 1 — REAR DECK

French doors from the house open directly onto a large, simple rectangular deck.

The deck is essentially a large outdoor porch. It is directly adjacent to the house and serves as the primary transition between the interior living spaces and the backyard.

The deck is surrounded by rock and flower gardens.

Straight ahead from the deck is a stone pathway leading through the gardens toward the lower pool area.

LAYER 2 — STONE PATH / GARDEN DESCENT

A stone path begins at the rear deck and continues approximately 20 feet through the surrounding rock and flower gardens.

The path gradually descends approximately 4 feet from the level of the rear deck to the pool deck.

The path provides the primary pedestrian route from the house-level deck to the pool area.

The transition between the upper deck and lower pool area is gradual rather than a sudden drop.

LAYER 3 — POOL DECK / POOL AREA

At the end of the descending stone path is the pool deck, approximately 4 feet below the level of the rear deck.

The pool deck and swimming pool form the third layer of the backyard.

The swimming pool is a large in-ground pool with wide entry steps and a diving board.

An in-ground hot tub is directly connected to the swimming pool.

The pool and hot tub are surrounded by a rough stone deck.

The pool deck contains lounge chairs, tables, and umbrellas.

A detached structure sits to the right of the pool area.

This structure serves three functions:

- Changing room
- Restroom
- Pool equipment and pump room

The structure is part of the pool area but is separate from the main house.

LAYER 4 — GARDENS / PATHS / RECREATION AREAS

The entire pool area is surrounded by extensive rock and flower gardens.

A network of stone paths winds through these gardens, allowing people to walk around the backyard rather than simply moving directly between the house and pool.

The paths are deliberately winding and maze-like.

Hedges and dense plantings are incorporated into the garden paths, creating partially enclosed passages and a maze-like feeling without completely isolating the different areas.

Throughout this garden area are various small spaces intended for sitting and relaxing, including areas with benches.

One dedicated area contains a picnic table and a gas grill.

The garden paths allow the backyard to be explored rather than functioning as a simple open yard surrounding the pool.

LAYER 5 — PERIMETER HEDGEROW

The entire backyard proper is enclosed by a tall, dense hedgerow.

The hedgerow forms a clear boundary between the maintained backyard and the surrounding forest.

It provides privacy and helps keep wildlife and unwanted people from entering the backyard.

The hedgerow surrounds all of the previous backyard layers, including the rear deck, descending path, pool area, and garden paths.

There is no grass in the backyard.

LAYER 6 — SURROUNDING FOREST

Beyond the perimeter hedgerow is the surrounding dense hardwood forest.

The forest is the natural environment surrounding the House and property.

The maintained backyard ends at the hedgerow; the forest beyond it is not part of the landscaped backyard.

HOUSE AI / FRONT DOOR

The House has an AI-controlled front door.

The House AI can identify people who are known and authorized to Bill.

When a known and authorized person arrives at the front door, the AI automatically unlocks and opens the front door for them.

After the person enters the House, the AI automatically closes the front door.

The AI does not automatically open the front door for unknown or unauthorized visitors. Unknown visitors require Bill's approval before the door is opened.

Only the front door is AI-controlled.

The AI does not control interior doors, garage doors, bedroom doors, the backyard gate, or other doors unless explicitly established elsewhere.
